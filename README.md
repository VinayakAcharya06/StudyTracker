StudyTrack ProStudyTrack Pro is a lightweight, local-first study tracker and spaced repetition app built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It keeps flashcards, lecture slide references, chapter notes, and practice tasks in one place without requiring external libraries, build tools, or cloud accounts.   FeaturesFour Study Formats: Add Flashcards (Q&A), Topic Notes (chapter summaries), Slide Decks (file names and slide numbers), and Practice Tasks (difficulty and deadlines).   Spaced Repetition: Rate your recall from 1 (Forgot) to 5 (Perfect). The app calculates your next review date automatically—ranging from 1 day for missed items up to 7+ days (scaled by your review count) for mastered ones.   Keyboard-First Review Mode: Flip 3D cards with Space and grade your recall with keys 1 through 5.   Focus Timer: Built-in Pomodoro timer with 25-minute work intervals, 5-minute short breaks, and 15-minute long breaks.   Progress Analytics: Track your daily study streak, average recall score, mastery breakdown, and item distribution by subject.   Local Storage & JSON Backups: Data saves automatically to your browser's localStorage. You can export your database to a .json file or import an existing backup from the settings menu.
